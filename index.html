The Three Pillars

Music • Sports • Media
01

Rap & Music

An independent lyricist, Knox translates the competitive drive of sports into his music. Known for projects like Samurai X, he pairs fast-paced flows with high-level production, frequently blending his music with sports culture on TikTok and X.

02

Soccer Journey

Before the mic, there was the pitch. Knox played professionally across the globe, with notable stints in Canada, Spain, Italy, Malta, and AFC Fylde in England. An ankle injury forced an early retirement, creating the pivot to his current multi-faceted career.

03

Ex-Pro Analysis

Leveraging his real playing experience, Knox provides deep-dive YouTube breakdowns of players like Messi and Ronaldo. He also collaborates with the Goluremi training platform, creating music and content that bridges entertainment and tactical education.
